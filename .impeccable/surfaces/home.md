# Surface: home (`/`)

Mode: Experience. Scope: the whole one-page showcase plus shared chrome (nav, footer); `/cv` inherits the world.

Audience and job: a technical decision-maker checks in a minute whether this engineer ships real products. Actions: open a project, get the CV, write an email.

Constraints: owner-pinned direction (holographic cyberpunk, 3D, GSAP); short copy; ES/EN; private client data masked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a hologram plate. A holographic plate stores an object as interference fringes and gives it back when light hits it; here every background is those fringes and the subject (the engineer, then each product) is reconstructed out of them. Refused: the neon-on-black terminal portfolio with a typing headline, a card grid of projects and a skills progress-bar wall.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is a blue-black ink, never pure black. Light comes in three named roles: Holo (cyan-mint, the reconstruction beam: links, focus, live state), Flare (hot magenta-red: the one primary action and active markers) and Sodium (amber: secondary data). Foil is the full diffraction spectrum, used only as a material on the name, on slate edges and on the 3D core. Type: Archivo across its width axis (expanded black for display, normal for text), Martian Mono for data and measurements only, Doto dot-matrix for numeric readouts. Components are slates: flat panels with 1px foil edges, square corners with a 2px ease, registration ticks at corners. No glass cards, no glow halos.

STORY: I see him appear out of light; I see six real products running, one per screen, each with one sentence and three facts; I see where he works now; I can reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name in two stacked lines of expanded black capitals, left-aligned, spanning about two thirds of the width at roughly 13vw. In front of the letters, right of centre, the portrait reconstructed as a 3D hologram (depth-displaced, scanlined, assembling from points on load) standing on a projector ring, partly occluding the type. Under the name: one line saying what he does, the location, and the primary action "Ver proyectos" in Flare with "CV" as the quiet second. Interference fringes fill the ground and bend toward the pointer.

FORM: Hologram plate / reconstruction. No direction roll was run: the owner's brief pinned the world (holographic cyberpunk, 3D, GSAP) and delegated art direction, and the launcher that runs the roll was declined in this session. seed: none (no roll run)

Signature interaction: the pinned project stage. Scrolling advances one product per step; a reconstruction beam scans the slate top to bottom and the screenshot of the next product resolves behind it while the fringe field shifts to that product's hue. Motion grammar: things resolve (scan, decode, converge) rather than slide or bounce; strong ease-out, UI feedback under 200ms, scroll scenes scrubbed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
